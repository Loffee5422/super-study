# Behavioral interview track

This is a minimal adapter built on the common mastery protocol. Use it for evidence-based rehearsal of collaboration, conflict, ambiguity, failure, leadership, prioritization, and growth.

## Inputs

Use the target job description to extract behavioral signals. Reuse a small bank of real experiences rather than inventing a new story for every question.

## Dimensions

- situation and stakes are understandable;
- the learner's task and ownership are explicit;
- actions contain concrete decisions and reasoning;
- collaboration and disagreement are represented honestly;
- result is specific and supported where possible;
- reflection shows learning and changed future behavior;
- follow-up answers remain consistent;
- the story maps to the job requirement without sounding memorized.

## Verification

First test factual completeness. Then test alternative decisions, personal contribution, negative consequences, stakeholder perspectives, and lessons. Help with structure only after the underlying evidence is sound.

Do not fabricate metrics, roles, conflicts, or outcomes. Mark sensitive details for anonymization in the final answer rather than changing the facts.
